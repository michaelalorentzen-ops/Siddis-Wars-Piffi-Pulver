# Stavanger-illustrasjoner

Laget med det innebygde imagegen-verktøyet. Originale PNG-filer er arkivert blant leveransene; lenkene her peker på de komprimerte WebP-filene som brukes i spillet. Illustrasjonene er stemningsbilder inspirert av områdene, ikke dokumentariske gjengivelser eller navigasjonskart.

## industry

Spillillustrasjon: [coast-industry.webp](../dist/assets/coast-industry.webp)

### Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: wide landscape narrative illustration for the Norwegian browser strategy game VESTKYST, Stavanger 2026–2036. Create a standalone full-bleed 1536x1024 illustration. Art direction: cinematic Nordic painted realism, finely drawn architectural detail, subtle print grain, deep midnight navy and slate teal shadows, warm restrained amber/brass practical lights, weathered materials and wet reflections. A coherent premium story-game illustration, not a UI mockup. No lettering, logos, captions, borders, watermark, weapons or illegal activity. Scene must be plausible for its specific area, not a collage of famous Norwegian landmarks. No need for blank title text; UI overlays will be separate. Scene: the working waterfront on Buøy in Stavanger, inspired by the Rosenberg industrial yard at blue hour. Large low fabrication halls and prefabrication workshops, cranes, dry-dock edges and deepwater quays, a service vessel moored at the quay, industrial lights reflected in dark calm fjord water. Ordinary workers in high-visibility rain clothing walking home after shift in foreground, viewed respectfully from a public waterfront. Emphasize the dignity and scale of shipbuilding/engineering work, modern industrial silhouettes rather than tourist wooden waterfront. Distant Stavanger city lights across the fjord. No company branding or readable signs. No offshore oil platform standing in the city, no giant cruise liner, no invented mountains in central Stavanger. Fine drizzle, realistic low coastline.
